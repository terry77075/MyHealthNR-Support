Privacy questions

Read the privacy policy or email us with the subject “My Health: NR privacy.”

What to include

  • Your phone or tablet model and operating-system version.
  • The app version and build number shown on the About screen or store listing.
  • What you were doing, what you expected, and the exact error message.
  • For Bluetooth questions, the health-device make and model.

Hide personal information in screenshots. Do not send passwords, full device serial numbers, or unnecessary health records.

Common questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. NR means No Registration. The app is designed to work without a developer-hosted account.

Where is my health information stored?

The information you enter or import is kept in an encrypted local database on your device. My Health: NR does not operate its own health-data cloud.

Can the developer recover my records?

No. The developer cannot remotely retrieve health records that exist only on your device and does not hold a recovery copy. Review the privacy policy before deleting or offloading the app.

Why can’t I connect my Bluetooth device?

Check that Bluetooth permission is enabled, wake the health device, and keep it nearby. Compatibility depends on the exact model and measurement protocol; discovering a device does not guarantee that readings can be imported. Manual entry remains available.

Are reminders guaranteed to appear?

No. The app can schedule local notifications, but delivery depends on notification permission, device settings, battery-management behavior, and the operating system. Keep important care instructions somewhere you can reliably access them.

How do medication name suggestions work?

The app uses a bundled offline list of common medication, vitamin, and supplement names. Suggestions are only entry aids, not medical recommendations. You can type another name as free text.

Can I share a report?

In versions with reporting, choose the information and date range, review the result, then use the system share or print controls. Information leaves the app only when you choose a destination.

Can support answer medical questions?

No. Contact your clinician or pharmacist about diagnosis, treatment, dosing, or medication changes. My Health: NR support is for help with the app and is not an emergency or clinical service.

Safety note

My Health: NR is a personal recordkeeping and reminder tool. It does not diagnose conditions or recommend treatment. In an emergency, contact local emergency services.
