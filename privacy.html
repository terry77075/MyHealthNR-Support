Plain-language privacy

Privacy policy

My Health: NR is built for personal recordkeeping without a developer-hosted health-data account.

Effective: November 2, 2026Applies to: My Health: NRFeatures vary by installed version and platform

The short version: the app stores the health information you enter or import in its local database on your device. It does not automatically send your health records to the developer.

Information stored in the app

Depending on the features available in your version, My Health: NR may store measurements and dates, medication and supplement details, schedules, dose and inventory history, allergies, health-journal entries, vaccination records, visits, reminder settings, notes, profile names, and technical details for health devices you choose to connect.

This information is stored in a local SQLCipher-encrypted database. The database key is kept using the device’s secure-storage facilities. Optional biometric or passcode protection can help protect access to the app. My Health: NR does not receive your Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint, or other biometric template.

What the developer collects

The app does not require a registration account and does not operate a developer-hosted health-data cloud. It does not automatically upload your health records to the developer. It does not include advertising, third-party behavioral analytics, or cross-app tracking. We do not sell your personal information or use your health records for advertising.

Bluetooth, reminders, calendar, and files

Bluetooth permission is used to communicate with supported health devices you choose. Compatibility depends on the device model and measurement protocol. Notification permission is used for local reminders you configure. Reminder delivery depends on device settings and operating-system behavior and is not guaranteed.

If you choose to add a follow-up visit to a calendar, the app uses the calendar permission to write that event to the calendar you select. If your version includes a supported document import, you choose the file and review extracted information before saving it. Features and supported file formats vary by installed version.

Medication name suggestions

The app includes a bundled offline list of common medication, vitamin, and supplement names to make entry easier. Suggestions are not medical recommendations, do not confirm that an entry is correct, and do not replace advice from a clinician or pharmacist. Free-text entry remains available.

Sharing is your choice

Reports and exports leave the app only when you choose a share or print action and select a destination. Once you send information through Mail, Messages, Files, a printer, or another service, that destination’s privacy and retention practices apply. Review a report before sharing it.

Backups and third-party services

My Health: NR does not provide a developer-hosted backup or synchronization service. Device backups, cloud-drive services, keyboard dictation, app stores, beta-testing services, operating-system diagnostics, and destinations you select are controlled by their providers and your settings. They may process information independently under their own privacy policies.

Keeping and removing records

Records remain on your device until you remove them through an available app control or delete the app’s data. Deleting the app generally removes its local app data; offloading behavior and device backups are controlled by the operating system. Previously exported files, recipient copies, backups, and secure-storage items can have separate retention rules. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or erase records stored only on your device and does not hold a recovery copy.

Support requests

If you email support@myhealthnr.com, we receive your email address and the content and attachments you choose to send. We use that information to answer your request and troubleshoot the app. Support email is handled through Google Workspace. Correspondence may be retained as needed to resolve requests and maintain support records; you may contact us to request deletion of correspondence we control.

Please do not send passwords or unnecessary health information. If a screenshot helps explain an issue, hide personal details first.

This website

This website is designed without advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, registration forms, or cookies added by My Health: NR. It is hosted as a static site through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process basic technical request information such as IP address, browser information, and request time under its own privacy practices.

Children, medical use, and emergencies

My Health: NR is a personal recordkeeping and reminder tool. It does not diagnose conditions, recommend treatment, determine medication doses, or replace advice from a licensed clinician or pharmacist. It is not an emergency service.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and its effective date when these practices materially change. For privacy questions or requests, email support@myhealthnr.com with the subject “My Health: NR privacy.”
